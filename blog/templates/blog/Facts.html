Programming trivia: the term debugging became famous after Grace Hopper’s team found a real moth stuck in a relay of the Harvard Mark II in 1947—they literally “debugged” the machine. That’s still the job: reproduce the problem, fix the root cause, and leave the system clearer than you found it. The counters below are a lighthearted snapshot of impact and grind in the same spirit.

Happy clients — teammates, stakeholders, and users who trusted the delivery

Projects — features, modules, and releases shipped across products

Hours of support — deep focus on builds, reviews, and keeping production healthy

Hard workers — collaborators, mentees, and engineers who leveled up alongside the work
